Education Across Australia

Lets dive into the 2021 Australian educaiton shpere to find out what influences how poeple are educated and what they are choosing to be educated in.

Who is Graduating University?



Looking into which states have the highest percentage of people with bachelores degrees we can see that there is about a 10% variation. The largest difference being approximatley 9% between the ACT and NT.

What is even more interesting is how this data correlates to other factors, for example the most densly populated states are the ACT, then Vic, then NSW.

This suggests that factors like population density can seriously influence the type of education everyday Australians persue.
